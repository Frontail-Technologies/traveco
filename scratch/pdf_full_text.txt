GLOBAL VISA CHECKLIST — 195 COUNTRIES
Indian Ordinary Passport Holders | Tourist / Visitor Travel | 2026 Working Reference
Purpose: A master operating document for a travel/visa desk. It covers the 193 UN member states plus Palestine and Vatican City as commonly used
sovereign-country reference points. Requirements, fees, appointment availability and processing times can change without notice. Exact live government fees
should be checked before quoting a client.
Important source note: India’s Ministry of External Affairs publishes a current “Visa facility for Indian Nationals (ordinary passport holders)” reference covering
e-Visa, visa-free and visa-on-arrival facilities. The MEA page currently lists, among other examples, Australia, Japan, Indonesia, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand,
Singapore, South Korea, Thailand, Vietnam and many African destinations under electronic/arrival facilities. Ethiopia is specifically noted as offering e-Tourist Visa
and Visa on Arrival to Indian ordinary-passport holders, with e-Tourist processing generally taking 3 working days.

1. MASTER TOURIST-VISA DOCUMENT CHECKLIST
DocumentTypical requirement
Passport
Original passport; normally at least 6 months validity beyond travel; sufficient blank pages; previous passports where
relevant.
PhotographRecent photo exactly matching destination dimensions/background/biometric rules.
ApplicationCompleted online/offline application; signed wherever required.
Cover letterPurpose, dates, itinerary, funding, employment/business status and intention to return.
FlightsReturn/onward reservation; avoid irreversible purchases unless the destination specifically requires them.
AccommodationHotel confirmation, host invitation or other acceptable proof.
ItineraryCity-wise/day-wise plan where required or useful.
BankingRecent personal bank statements; normally 3–6 months depending on destination.
IncomeSalary slips, employment certificate, Form 16/ITR or business financial evidence.
Employment/businessLeave/NOC, HR letter, company registration, GST/business licence, business bank statements, etc.
InsuranceMandatory for Schengen and some other destinations; coverage rules are destination-specific.
Travel historyPrevious visas, entry/exit stamps and old passports where relevant.
Invitation/sponsorInvitation, sponsor identity/status, relationship and financial evidence where applicable.
Civil documentsMarriage/birth/relationship documents where relevant.
Translations/legalisationCertified translation, apostille/legalisation or notarisation where required.
Biometrics/medical/policeOnly where the destination/category requires it.

3. CASE-TYPE CHECKLISTS
Salaried applicant
Passport + photo + application + employer letter + approved leave/NOC + salary slips + personal bank statements + ITR/Form 16 + itinerary + hotel +
return/onward booking + insurance where required + cover letter + previous travel evidence.
Self-employed / business owner
Passport + application + company registration/GST/business licence + company and personal bank statements + ITR + ownership proof + business
cover letter + itinerary + hotel + invitation where relevant + return booking + insurance where required.
Sponsored applicant
Applicant core file + sponsor letter + sponsor passport/ID/residence status + sponsor financial evidence + relationship proof + accommodation +
undertaking where required.
Minor
Passport + application + birth certificate + parents’ IDs/passports + parental consent/NOC + custody order if relevant + school letter + sponsor/financial
documents.
Family group
Individual forms and passport documents for every applicant; common itinerary/accommodation; relationship evidence; consolidated sponsor/financial
documents where appropriate.
4. FEE-QUOTING FORMAT FOR YOUR TRAVEL AGENCY
Always quote separately: Government visa fee | Biometrics fee | VFS/BLS/TLS/outsourced service fee | Courier | SMS | Premium/Prime service |
Insurance | Translation/legalisation | Your professional/service fee. This prevents clients from confusing the government fee with agency charges.
